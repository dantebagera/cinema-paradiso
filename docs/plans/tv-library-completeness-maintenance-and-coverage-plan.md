# TV Library completeness, Maintenance, and episode coverage

**Status:** Full-scope, single-approval implementation proposal, updated 2026-09-27. Dante has authorized edits to this plan document and read-only investigation only. No application implementation, catalog change, metadata refresh, restart, commit, or release is authorized yet. Sections labeled **Agreed** record Dante's decisions; the remaining defaults in **Single-approval contract** are explicit proposals to be accepted or rejected together with one implementation approval.

## What is broken for Dante

1. A show can appear to be missing because the TV Library gives no clear total or way to distinguish a card farther down the list from an unidentified folder. The current default order puts South Park at card 7, below the captured viewport.
2. Unidentified TV folders are handled by a TV Library review dialog, while Maintenance's Identity view only reports movie-catalog problems. This violates the movie-style review location Dante expects.
3. A TV card mixes two different episode totals. The heading uses the provider's full series count; the availability line uses only episode rows currently loaded into CP. This makes coverage and any percentage misleading.
4. Specials, unaired episodes, missing metadata, and files whose identity remains in review do not have a clear shared counting contract.
5. The series page also displays **Monitoring and acquisition** without clearly separating automatic downloading from identification of files already on disk. Its controls can look like part of the library scan even though they serve a different purpose.
6. On the Library TV cards, the fixed-height poster can end above the bottom of a taller card, leaving obvious empty space. The `N episodes available` badge duplicates the availability line, the line lacks the agreed percentages, and the separate **Start** button consumes space that the movie-style over-poster Play control can use.
7. **Movie Lists** currently handles movies only. TV series watchlist/progress are stored separately, so renaming the navigation item alone would not make Watched, Watchlist, or custom lists work for TV.

The dropdown in Dante's screenshot has five choices. Their **current implementation**, not their implied names, is:

| Choice | What the monitor currently does |
| --- | --- |
| Not monitored | No automatic source search or submission. |
| future | Searches unavailable episodes already in CP whose air date is on or after the rule was saved, once released. It skips the existing backlog and cannot discover future episodes missing from the catalog. |

The currently configured CP Compatibility profile targets 1080p, uses move import, and does not seed after import; Safe HD also targets 1080p but uses hardlink/copy import and continued seeding. Neither enables automatic upgrades. A profile name alone does not explain these consequences.

## Product contract for the single approval

### Library visibility and file accounting

- Every supported video under configured TV roots receives one explainable state: linked to a known episode, known show but episode unresolved, show identity unresolved, excluded extra/non-TV item, or scan/probe error. CP must not silently drop a folder or file from all review surfaces.
- The TV Library states its total number of show cards and provides an obvious title search. A folder with unresolved identity appears in **Maintenance → TV Identity** with its file count, path, candidate shows, and the reason CP withheld an automatic match.
- A known show with unresolved episodes still has a show card. Its unresolved files appear in the same Maintenance TV queue, grouped under that show, rather than requiring Dante to hunt through every series page.
- An explicit exclusion state handles movies, extras, samples, and non-TV videos found under a TV root. Exclusions remain visible for audit and can be corrected.
- Search, sorting, filters, and pagination must never make a matching local show disappear without an accurate result count or an explanation that a filter is active.

### One episode-count definition

Main card line for Westworld after complete hydration: **`10 / 36 episodes offline (28%) · 0 / 36 watched (0%)`**. Dante agreed to both counts and percentages. Remove the separate `10 episodes available` chip because it repeats this line.

- **Total / denominator:** canonical regular episodes in released seasons, excluding Specials. The provider's series summary may be used as a temporary display estimate, but a percentage must not be presented as complete until CP has loaded and reconciled the episode list for every regular season. For an ended show like Westworld, the target denominator is 36, not the currently loaded 20.
- **Offline / numerator:** distinct regular episode identities with at least one verified, still-existing playable local file. Multiple copies of an episode count once. A file awaiting show or episode review does not count as an identified offline episode; its pending count remains visible in Maintenance.
- **Watched / numerator:** distinct regular episodes marked completed in CP. Show the watched count and percentage separately from offline coverage. Progress on a special does not inflate regular-season completion.
- **Specials:** display separately, for example `3 specials offline`, with their own counts if useful. Do not mix Specials into the main 36 or its percentage.
- **Unaired / unknown dates:** keep them distinct from aired regular episodes. The proposed single-approval default for a continuing show is released regular episodes as the denominator, with announced future episodes shown separately so the percentage does not fall merely because a future season is announced. Unknown dates cannot be silently treated as released.
- **Rounding:** nearest whole percentage, clamp to 0–100. Show `—` when the denominator is genuinely unknown or incomplete; do not substitute the number of loaded episodes and present it as the full series total.
- The same authoritative coverage projection must supply the Library card, series page, search filters/sort, and any Maintenance summary. JSX should format supplied values rather than recalculate them.

### Follow and download: agreed direction

This is a separate acquisition feature, not TV Library file identification. Dante's South Park example has two distinct actions: download already released episodes missing offline, and follow episodes that release later. The user may save both as separate tasks. The visible title is **Follow and download**, replacing **Monitoring and acquisition**. Saving creates a draft task row; its **Start** button begins the action. Merely choosing a dropdown value or saving a draft does not start downloading.

The TV Library's selected-card **Download** button is a different, one-shot action: it queues sources found for currently released missing episodes, reports any no-source episodes, and finishes without retry. It never creates a Follow task or watches for later episodes. A deliberately started **Follow and download → Missing** task retains the retry lifecycle described below.

| First selector / scope | Second selector / action | Intended behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whole series | Missing | Immediately search for and download the released episodes missing from this series when the task starts. Retry unavailable sources; do not include unreleased episodes. |
| Selected season | Missing | The same, limited to the chosen season. |
| Whole series | Future | Follow newly released episodes across this and later seasons. Start searching on or after each release date and retry until acquired. Finish when the series has ended and every eligible episode in scope is acquired. Do not offer Future for a series currently marked ended. |
| Selected season | Future | Follow only the chosen season, including future episodes added to its provider listing. Finish when that season is complete and all its eligible episodes are acquired. Do not wait for or include later seasons. |
| Whole series / selected season | Upgrade | Consider an owned episode only when a source can be proven better under the chosen quality profile. Keep the existing copy until its replacement is verified; do not redownload every owned episode by default. |
| Selected episodes | Download selected | Select episodes across any seasons. The second selector is disabled and dimmed because Missing/Future do not apply as a separate choice. Selected released episodes can start now; selected future episodes wait for their release dates. This must be reconciled with the existing per-season **Download selected** action rather than creating duplicate acquisition paths. |

**Task lifecycle:** A saved row shows scope, action, quality profile, target/progress counts, state, and **Start** and **X** controls as applicable. A started Missing or Upgrade task remains visible while searching, downloading, retrying, or needing review; it leaves the active list after its targets are acquired or the task is explicitly cancelled, while its outcome remains in history. A started Future task remains visible through successive episodes and completes only when its selected series or season is complete and all eligible episodes in that scope are acquired. A provider status change alone must not mark it complete while episodes remain missing. An unavailable release is a retrying/waiting state, not success. Proposed default if a provider later reverses `Ended`: retain the completed task in history and notify Dante that a new follow task is needed; do not silently resume a completed task.

**Series and season completion evidence:** TMDB provides a series status, season episode counts, and episode air dates. Its season detail does not provide a separate authoritative `ended` flag. Do not equate "all episodes currently listed have downloaded" with "this season is over" when its list may grow. A whole-series Future task refreshes metadata to learn newly announced seasons and episodes and completes only when the series is confirmed `Ended` and all eligible episodes are acquired. A selected-season Future task in an ongoing series enters **Waiting for season-end confirmation** once all known episodes are acquired; it does not guess that the season is finished. Dante can explicitly finish that task after seeing the refreshed evidence. If the whole series becomes `Ended`, the selected-season task can complete automatically once all its eligible episodes are acquired.

**Scope and existing controls:** `inherit` currently means a season/episode rule uses its parent monitoring rule. Do not expose that internal term beside Missing/Future/Upgrade. If season override editing still requires it, label it **Use series rule** and show the effective inherited action. Remove the redundant `all` choice after migrating existing saved rules safely. Rename the misleading `cutoff` UI label to **Upgrade** only after its backend behavior matches the agreed upgrade contract. Preserve current saved rules and acquisition history through migration. A separate quality/profile selector must explain source eligibility, replacement policy, import behavior, and seeding in plain language.

**Cancellation:** X opens a confirmation with three explicit choices: (1) stop searching and queued work but keep files; (2) also cancel in-progress transfers and delete their incomplete files; (3) also delete completed files acquired by this task. Show the affected transfers and exact paths before destructive choices. Files owned before the task, shared with another acquisition/show, or currently being played are excluded from removal and reported as protected. An imported file is removable only when the task has exclusive, durable ownership of that exact path; a shared torrent or uncertain ownership blocks file deletion while still allowing the search task to stop. Record the selected outcome, actual removals, and protected paths in durable task history.

**Notification window:** Add a desktop bell/window with unread count, severity, grouping by task/episode, links to the affected show/download/review, explicit read state, and searchable durable history without automatic expiry. Meaningful events include a new episode becoming eligible, a source found, transfer/import completion, retry because no source was found, a validation or identity problem needing action, task completion, and cancellation/deletion results. Only a meaningful state change creates a new unread event; a retry polling loop must not alert every cycle. Keep the event envelope typed by media and source so later automatic downloads for followed **movies** use this same surface while movie and TV catalog/acquisition ownership remains separate. Today's transient toast is insufficient.

**Acquisition safeguards:** Refresh monitored provider metadata on a bounded schedule; record last refresh, last search, next retry, and errors. Provider failure must preserve known episodes, file bindings, and watch history. Preview missing, future, owned, already queued, and unresolved-file counts before saving. Episodes with unresolved local files are withheld from automatic acquisition pending review so CP does not duplicate an existing copy; unaffected episodes in the task may proceed. Continue using the existing TV identity/conflict checks, source validation, per-episode intents, and qBittorrent transfer owner. Exclude Specials from whole-series/season Missing, Future, Upgrade, and bulk Download by default; allow them only through explicit episode selection.

### Follow and download: defaults included in one approval

- **Upgrade:** Compare an owned episode with candidates under the selected quality profile using verified local file facts and source evidence. Queue only a strictly better eligible release. A tie, missing comparison facts, or merely different filename is not an upgrade; report it as ineligible/uncertain. Preserve the old copy until the replacement is validated and imported. This implements Dante's better-only decision.
- **Missing snapshot:** Refresh provider metadata before Start, then freeze the task's released, missing regular-episode targets. A later discovered episode appears in a new preview/notification but does not silently expand this Missing task or convert it into Future.
- **Selected episodes:** One parent task can hold explicitly selected episodes across seasons with durable per-episode intents. An owned episode is skipped unless Dante explicitly chooses Upgrade. A selected future episode waits for its air date; this fixed selection does not become a whole-series Future rule.
- **Season end and cancellation:** Use the uncertainty and protected-file behavior specified above; never infer season completion from the current count alone or delete a shared/playing file.

## Zero-known-regression acceptance gate

**Acceptance rule:** zero known new regressions and zero unexplained failures at live cutover. This is a strict release gate, not a claim that software can be proved free of every future bug. Any new failure, unexplained intermittent failure, broken existing behavior, or unresolved data discrepancy blocks the live cutover. Do not weaken or remove a test, relax a timing threshold, or describe a focused rerun as a green full suite to get past the gate.

1. **Baseline before source changes:** recheck the Desktop checkout, dirty paths, configuration/data owner, running process, port 5000, and served `dist`. Record the existing full Python, Node, and desktop Playwright results and exact test counts, plus a production build. Preserve the baseline logs and failure artifacts. Classify any existing failure with reproduction and evidence before attributing a later failure to this implementation. Every full-suite gate must be clean before live cutover; a known or intermittent baseline failure remains red until fixed and verified. If fixing it materially expands the approved scope, stop the cutover and report that boundary.
2. **Isolated execution:** run Python tests with `CP_TEST_MODE=1` and a unique OS-temp `CP_TEST_ROOT`; use the existing guarded `.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest discover -s tests -p "test_*.py"` path. ## Risks to address in the design

- Loading all seasons can require many provider requests for long-running shows such as South Park. The job needs bounds, caching, progress, and retry without blocking page loads.
- Provider episode numbering and air dates can change. Metadata refresh must preserve local file bindings and watched history and flag reconciliation conflicts rather than silently move files to another episode.
- An aggressive matcher can turn an uncertain show into the wrong card. Uncertain identities belong in Maintenance with evidence, not an automatic guess.
- Adding TV to Maintenance can accidentally disturb movie review behavior if the existing movie audit is expanded directly. Keep separate domain services and test both surfaces.
- Percentages can change as a continuing show airs new episodes. The card must state the denominator policy clearly.
