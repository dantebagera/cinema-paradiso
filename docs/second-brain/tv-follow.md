## Approved behavior

| Decision | Rule and reason | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Save and Start | On the desktop Follow panel, Preview shows the targets, Save creates a draft, and Start on that draft activates the task. This gives Dante a chance to review scope and blockers before acquisition. | [TV-FOLLOW-01](../tv-contracts.md#existing-follow-and-download-contracts) |
| Missing, Future, and Upgrade | Missing starts with a fixed backlog of released missing regular episodes in the chosen series or season. Future follows newly eligible releases within its scope. Upgrade needs a provably better eligible source and protects the owned copy until the replacement is verified. Selected episodes can span seasons, and an unreleased selection waits. | [TV-FOLLOW-02 through 05](../tv-contracts.md#existing-follow-and-download-contracts) |
| First search and retries | Dante settled **five hours after an unsuccessful source search** for persistent Follow Missing and Future on 2026-10-08. The first Missing search is due on the next eligible worker pass after Start. Future is eligible at midnight Africa/Cairo on the listed air date, or on the next eligible pass if CP was offline. An unknown date waits for metadata. This keeps the first search prompt and avoids replaying missed intervals. | [Settled timing](../tv-contracts.md#first-search-timing-discussion) |
| Season packs | Dante approved season-first searching on 2026-10-10 for eligible whole-series or season Missing tasks when every regular episode in the season is released, missing, in scope, and free of conflicts. Quality comes first and coverage breaks ties. An eligible complete pack can cover other authorized episodes in the same season before their individual retry times. This lets one pack fill a season while preserving episode fallback and each target's retry clock. | [TV-CHANGE-05 P1](../tv-contracts.md#tv-change-05-season-first-missing-and-truthful-source-review) |
| Source and transfer review | A candidate needing deliberate identity or quality confirmation is shown as **needs review** with no transfer intent and stays on the five-hour source retry. The manual review applies to the selected release. Hard identity conflicts stay blocked. If a transfer or import needs review after submission, CP keeps its intent for recovery. This preserves validation and avoids duplicate work. | [TV-CHANGE-05 P2](../tv-contracts.md#tv-change-05-season-first-missing-and-truthful-source-review) |
| Other download controls | Lower **Download all seasons** or checked-season controls open manual release review, with queuing after explicit submission. TV Library bulk Download runs once for released missing episodes and reports unavailable sources. Each gives Dante a way to download without setting up continuing Follow. | [Lower controls](../tv-contracts.md#tv-change-02-clear-activation-and-downloads-across-seasons) · [Library bulk and retry boundary](../tv-contracts.md#tv-change-04-new-release-following-versus-downloading-an-existing-series) |
| Finish or cancel | An ongoing show's season-scoped Future task can require explicit season-end confirmation. Cancellation has a preview and a separate choice for eligible files. Existing library media stays protected by the cancellation rules. | [TV-FOLLOW-03 and 07](../tv-contracts.md#existing-follow-and-download-contracts) · [Cancellation and lifecycle plan](../plans/tv-library-completeness-maintenance-and-coverage-plan.md) |

## Superseded and open items

- **Superseded** · Six hours was an early proposal for unsuccessful source searches. The same discussion also recorded a historical one-hour source retry in the old code. The agreed rule is five hours for persistent Missing and Future. Future's separate six-hour metadata refresh is still part of the recorded design. [Timing discussion and resolution](../tv-contracts.md#tv-change-04-new-release-following-versus-downloading-an-existing-series)
- **Deferred** · A combined Missing-plus-Future action and extended Future scope remain deferred. The existing data supports date-only eligibility. A new schedule provider or a timestamp-based policy needs its own decision. [Decision state](../tv-contracts.md#tv-change-04-new-release-following-versus-downloading-an-existing-series)
