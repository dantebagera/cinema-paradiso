## Query, data, and failure boundaries

- The shared `AdvancedSearchBuilder` and `advancedSearchModel.js` produce typed criteria. Backend `normalize_query` validates the domain and scope, group joins, controlled identities, numeric bounds, and limits. Movie Library applies them against saved facts in the catalog. Movie Discover uses the TMDB key and provider summaries or bounded detail checks, with local catalog ownership where needed. [Builder](../../src/features/search/AdvancedSearchBuilder.jsx) · [Frontend model](../../src/features/search/advancedSearchModel.js) · [Backend model](../../services/advanced_search.py) · [Movie SQL](../../services/catalog_store.py) · [Planner](../../services/tmdb_advanced_search.py)
- Movie Discover uses a transient ordered query snapshot to deduplicate, filter, and fill before slicing. It can return an unknown total or a retryable incomplete scan. The workspace uses an abort controller and request sequence so old results or errors cannot overwrite current results. Upcoming shares `_tmdb_filter_upcoming_movies` and the date-aware provider query with Home. [Window, snapshots, and Upcoming rule](../../app.py) · [Request and rendering](../../src/features/discover/DiscoverWorkspace.jsx) · [Home map](home-lists-and-notifications.md)
- TV Library uses saved series and coverage in `tv-catalog.sqlite`. TV Discover combines its local read context with TMDB `/search/tv`, feeds, `/discover/tv`, or person TV credits. `TVSearch._discover_page` keeps unfinished detail candidates for a retry in bounded filtered scans. `TVWorkspace` enriches returned cards through `/api/tv/catalog/enrich`, with priority for visible cards. Follow badges come from the monitoring projection. [Search](../../services/tv_search.py) · [TV service composition](../../services/tv_services.py) · [TV routes](../../services/tv_routes.py) · [Card enrichment](../../src/features/tv/tvCardEnrichment.js)
