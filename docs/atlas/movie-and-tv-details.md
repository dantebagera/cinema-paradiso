## Expanded details and credits

| Surface | Execution path |
| --- | --- |
| Expanded Movie card | Library, Discover, Movie Lists, and Home use shared Movie details and card components. `LibraryMovieCard` and `DiscoverMovieCard` use `UnifiedMovieCard` for the shell and `MovieExpandedDetails` for credits and related tiles. `fetchCanonicalMovieDetails` reads owned details from `/api/library/details` by path and merges any provider enrichment while preserving accepted local facts. Provider-only movies use `/api/tmdb/details` by TMDB ID. A Plex-only movie without the ID can use a strict title/year resolution before fetching provider details. [Shared cards](../../src/components/SharedMovieCards.jsx) · [Card shell](../../src/components/movie-card/MovieCard.jsx) · [Details client](../../src/api/movieDetails.js) · [Routes](../../app.py) |
| Movie language and biography | `useTransientMovieLanguage` overlays display fields from `/api/tmdb/details?language=ar-SA` and restores canonical English. `usePersonBiography` fetches `/api/tmdb/person`. The standard details route caches qualifying English provider projections and keeps the Arabic response transient. Catalog-generation refresh marks cached Movie details stale while keeping visible data. [Language owner](../../src/components/MovieLanguageToggle.jsx) · [Biography](../../src/components/PersonCreditCard.jsx) · [Details client](../../src/api/movieDetails.js) · [Provider route](../../app.py) |
| Expanded TV series and season | `TVSeriesPage` chooses saved `/api/tv/series/<id>` and its `/selection` route, or transient `/api/tv/preview/<tmdb_id>` and its season route. Saved reads use `TVReadModel.detail` in `tv-catalog.sqlite`. The route can fill display metadata for older saved rows through `TVMetadata.preview`. Preview remains transient until an explicit Add to library. Temporary Arabic comes from `/api/tv/translation/<tmdb_id>`. [Series page](../../src/features/tv/TVWorkspace.jsx) · [TV routes](../../services/tv_routes.py) · [Read model](../../services/tv_read_model.py) · [Provider metadata](../../services/tv_metadata.py) |
| Movie and TV credits | `PersonCreditCard` provides biography and distinct browse/Filmography actions. Movie Discover uses `/api/tmdb/person_movies` or a refined context through `loadContextPage`. The full-filmography button clears the destination's current criteria and preserves the origin for Back. `TVSeriesCredits` groups TV credits, fills missing portraits through the person endpoint, and opens a TV person query through `/api/tv/catalog/search`. TV navigation retains the selected season. [Credit component](../../src/components/PersonCreditCard.jsx) · [Movie navigation](../../src/features/discover/DiscoverWorkspace.jsx) · [TV credits](../../src/features/tv/TVSeriesCredits.jsx) · [TV navigation](../../src/features/tv/TVWorkspace.jsx) |

## Collections, related, awards, and artwork

| Feature | Owner, persistence, and failure path |
| --- | --- |
| Movie collections | `MovieExpandedCuration` and `useMovieCollectionCache` show members or counts. `movieCollectionUrl` chooses `/api/library/collection/<id>` for SQL Library details or `/api/tmdb/collection` for provider details and keeps the caches distinct. `_effective_tmdb_collection` combines provider parts with collection overrides in the `user_collections.json` catalog document. The Library route resolves owned paths, unresolved parts, and conflicts against catalog identity and file presence. Discover collection navigation clears the previous title search, preserves deliberate refinements, and saves the origin. Provider-empty, filter-empty, and failure have distinct outcomes. [Shared card](../../src/components/SharedMovieCards.jsx) · [Cache](../../src/hooks/useMovieCollectionCache.js) · [URL selection](../../src/api/movieDetails.js) · [Routes](../../app.py) · [Curation store](../../services/curation_store.py) |
| Related titles | `RelatedMovieRail` uses `fetchRelationshipPage` or `fetchTvRelationshipPage`. `/api/tmdb/related` and `/api/tv/related` use separate instances of `MovieRelationships` with domain-specific providers. TV adds TV-local IDs and availability. Movie View all loads its page and bounded summary independently, so a count error keeps the cards usable. The service caches provider pages in memory and deduplicates by identity. Inspect source choice and history separately from ownership or display projection. [Rail](../../src/components/RelatedMovieRail.jsx) · [Clients](../../src/api/movieRelationships.js) · [Movie route](../../app.py) · [TV route](../../services/tv_routes.py) · [Relationship service](../../services/movie_relationships.py) |
| Movie awards | `MovieAwardsProvider` loads a generation-aware snapshot for opted-in Movie surfaces. `MovieAwardsStore` owns verified counts and role-specific winning-film lists in `awards/awards.sqlite` under the configured data root. `register_movie_awards_routes` serves snapshot, status, and person-films. A missing or unavailable snapshot leaves ordinary details usable. Person-award clicks open Discover winning films and preserve origin history. The store and refresh service validate and publish ceremony contributions separately from normal Movie details. [Provider](../../src/components/MovieAwardsContext.jsx) · [Client](../../src/api/movieAwards.js) · [Routes](../../services/movie_awards_routes.py) · [Store](../../services/movie_awards_store.py) · [Refresh](../../services/movie_awards_refresh.py) |
| Poster choice and local artwork | `PosterEditorModal` uses `/api/library/posters` and its select, upload, and reset endpoints. `AppMetadataStore` owns Movie overrides and `MediaAssetService` serves checksum-backed local assets via `/api/assets/<checksum>`. TV `TVPosterEditor` posts or deletes `/api/tv/series/<id>/poster`; `TVCatalogRepository` owns the saved TV override. `cardArtwork.js` and `UnifiedMovieCard` own when artwork is requested or painted. The hooks load expanded artwork immediately and can defer distant cards until visible. [Movie editor](../../src/components/PosterEditorModal.jsx) · [Routes](../../app.py) · [Assets](../../services/media_assets.py) · [TV editor](../../src/features/tv/TVPosterEditor.jsx) · [TV store](../../services/tv_catalog_repository.py) · [Card artwork](../../src/components/movie-card/cardArtwork.js) |
