The Library route resolves owned paths, unresolved parts, and conflicts against catalog identity and file presence. Discover collection navigation clears the previous title search, preserves deliberate refinements, and saves the origin. Provider-empty, filter-empty, and failure have distinct outcomes. The service caches provider pages in memory and deduplicates by identity. Inspect source choice and history separately from ownership or display projection. [Rail](../../src/components/RelatedMovieRail.jsx) · [Clients](../../src/api/movieRelationships.js) · [Movie route](../../app.py) · [TV route](../../services/tv_routes.py) · [Relationship service](../../services/movie_relationships.py) |
